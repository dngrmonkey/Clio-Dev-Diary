## AJ's Objective

AJ wanted to bring order to a growing ChatGPT working environment without simply moving or deleting conversations until the interface looked cleaner.

The underlying problem was broader than chat cleanup. Different kinds of work were accumulating in places that did not necessarily match their purpose, persistence needs, or ownership. The goal became establishing a repeatable way to decide what each item was and where it belonged before reorganizing it.

## Work Completed

Metis developed a classification approach for evaluating AJ's existing ChatGPT environment.

The work distinguished among several different kinds of working structures, including Projects, individual chats, Work or artifact environments, reusable assistants or GPTs, agents or multi-step workflows, and external repositories or other durable sources of truth.

A practical review model also emerged around actions such as keeping, moving, merging, converting, extracting, archiving, or deleting material.

The central rule was to classify an item before changing its location or disposition rather than migrating the environment blindly.

The work also identified ChatGPT's Recents interface as insufficient by itself for durable organization. A visible conversation list could help locate recent activity, but it was not treated as the governing structure for AJ's project portfolio.

## Decisions and Why They Matter

AJ's environment would be reorganized according to the purpose and persistence requirements of the work rather than according to where conversations happened to exist.

Classification would come before moving or deleting.

The work also recognized conversation context limits as an architectural constraint. Long-running work should not depend indefinitely on one increasingly large conversation retaining all relevant state. Current-state or continuation documents were identified as a way to preserve important context across working sessions.

Together, these decisions changed the problem from a one-time interface cleanup into a repeatable portfolio-governance process.

## Milestones Reached

A structured model existed for evaluating the different kinds of work in AJ's ChatGPT environment and determining their appropriate ownership and persistence.

The need for durable state outside indefinitely growing conversation history was also explicitly recognized.
